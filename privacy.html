This privacy policy explains how Allyson Renee (the photographer) handles information and photos related to Glendale Highschool Marching Band student portrait orders and the online gallery.

Photos & advertising

Student photos will not be used for advertisement, marketing, or promotional purposes without specific written consent from a parent or guardian.

Photos are created for the school portrait program and for families who purchase packages or digital rights. Personal-use downloads and prints are for family and school-related use only and may not be sold or used commercially by purchasers.

Information we collect

When you place an order or contact us, we may collect:

  • Student name, grade, and instrument/section
  • Parent/guardian name, phone, and email
  • Gallery access code (if provided)
  • Order details (package, add-ons, and totals)
  • Payment confirmation details from Stripe (we do not store full card numbers on this site)
  • Messages you send through the question form below

How we use information

  • To fulfill print and digital orders
  • To unlock gallery downloads after digital rights are paid
  • To communicate about orders, delivery, or support
  • To answer questions you send through this page

We do not sell your personal information.

Payments

Online card payments are processed by Stripe. Stripe’s privacy practices apply to payment data they handle. Order confirmation details may be emailed to the photographer so the order can be completed.

Gallery access

Access codes are intended for the student’s family. Please keep codes private. Preview images may be watermarked until digital rights are purchased.

Contact

For privacy questions or to request written consent documentation for promotional use of a photo, use the form below or email Allyson Renee.
